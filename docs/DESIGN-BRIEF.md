# EXCEED Dubai 営業資料 v20 — Design Edit Brief (source of truth)

Context: live investor seminar in Japan, developer conference **with DAMAC**.
Audience: Japanese retail investors. **Projected in a room** — ~10 seconds per slide,
must survive a projector from the back. Density is the enemy.

## Standing rules
1. **Additive only.** Content may be added / clarified / reordered / re-weighted.
   Removal needs Baru's explicit OK first. Design changes: free rein.
2. **Dual output.** `slides/` = main deck (edits in place, structure preserved).
   `alt/` = alternatives deck of suggested variants for the presenter to choose from.
3. **Suggested slides ship finished** — content variant + its design direction together.
4. **Never guess assets.** Ask Baru for every image/logo. No web placeholders, no AI
   substitutes unless explicitly told.
5. **Projector font floor** enforced deck-wide; flag any slide that breaks it.

## Design system
- **Photography / data alternation is deliberate.** Never put photography behind a data
  slide — it flattens pace and kills projector contrast.
- **Spotlight glow: blue-background slides only.** Never on full-bleed photo slides or
  chapter dividers. **Vary its position slide to slide** — a motif with movement, not a stamp.
- **Monogram lives inside the spotlight only.** Same hue, very low contrast. The icon must be
  **topical to that slide**. Never reuse a motif. **Skip it on dense slides** (22, 23) —
  spotlight only.
